[1] Title: Season Resumes Behind Closed Doors, Day: August 13, 2020 Content: Rowan Rovers began the new campaign last night, the season resuming on 12 August 2020 in an empty stadium as restrictions held. The club's accounts show the cost of the silent terraces, and the chairman has repeated his appeal for a supporters' support fund. The manager, long-serving Dieter Weill, named three academy players in the squad, one of them scoring on debut. The league has published its testing figures weekly, and Rovers' camp has recorded no positive results since camp opened. Season ticket holders will be credited against next term. The academy's under-eighteens begin their own campaign next month, and the club's community programme has delivered meals through the close season. Transfer business has been quiet, the chairman citing the uncertainty, though the squad's wage deferral arrangement ends this month.
[2] Title: Weill Sacked as Rovers Slide, Day: November 24, 2020 Content: Rowan Rovers have parted company with manager Dieter Weill, the dismissal announced on 23 November 2020 after a run of one win in nine. The club moved quickly to steady the dressing room: a caretaker was appointed on 24 November 2020, academy director Sal Ortega taking charge for the foreseeable future. Weill leaves after six seasons, the longest reign in the club's modern history, having kept the club in the division on a shoestring. The chairman thanked him in a statement that cleared the way for a January search for a permanent successor. Fans' groups reacted with sadness rather than anger, pointing to the sale of the club's two leading forwards in the close season. The team sits four points above the relegation places with a game in hand. Ortega's first match in charge ends the month.
[3] Title: Rovers Seal Promotion at Last, Day: May 9, 2021 Content: Rowan Rovers sealed promotion yesterday, 8 May 2021, with a win that ends the club's nine-year wait. The promotion, achieved under caretaker-turned-permanent manager Sal Ortega, was crowned by the cup run that began with a tie won on 9 January 2021 against the division leaders. The January window brought the club's transfer record, a fee of one and a quarter million paid on 1 February 2021 for the striker whose goals settled the run-in. The team coach will tour the town on Monday, and the parade has been fixed for 10 May 2021, the town's first such celebration within living memory. The chairman promised the permanent stand repairs the fans have long demanded. Ortega, asked about his own future, confirmed he has signed the two-year deal offered in March. The players' bonus pool, agreed in February, will be honoured in full.

[1] Title: Meridian Cycling Signs Sprint Prospect, Day: September 15, 2022 Content: The Meridian cycling team has signed the sprinter Kalle Roon on a three-year contract, the signature completed on 14 September 2022 after a summer of negotiation between his agents and the team's management. Roon, twenty-three, comes from the continental development ranks with a season of eleven wins. The team's manager called him the fastest finisher his squad has employed. Roon will make his debut at the season opener, the squad's first race falling on 22 January 2023 in the gulf tour's opening stage. The signing completes a two-year rebuilding begun when the team's classification leader retired. Roon's equipment contract includes the new aero frame, and his lead-out train has been retained in full. The team's sponsors have extended their backing through the Olympic cycle, the announcement accompanying the rider's unveiling at the team's headquarters.
[2] Title: Roon Fractures Collarbone in Flanders Fall, Day: March 19, 2023 Content: Kalle Roon fractured his collarbone in a fall at the Flemish classic on 18 March 2023, abandoning the race at the cobbled sector with ten kilometres ridden. The sprinter was released from hospital on 21 March 2023, the pin inserted in the bone, and his recovery is put at four weeks. The team says his spring programme is over but the summer remains possible. The crash, caused by a touch of wheels in the bunch, took down six riders, none of the others seriously hurt. Roon's debut season had begun promisingly, his first race for the team having come at the gulf tour in January. Team management thanked the race doctor and the hospital's staff. The rider himself posted a message of thanks from his hospital bed, and the team's mechanics have rebuilt his machines for the return.
[3] Title: Roon Returns and Extends, Day: October 8, 2023 Content: Kalle Roon returned to competition on 15 April 2023, four weeks after his collarbone surgery, finishing the regional tour in the bunch and claiming a top ten in its final sprint. His season gathered pace thereafter, and on 6 October 2023 the team announced his contract extension by two further years, tying him to Meridian through 2027. The extension follows his stage wins in the summer, three in the ocean race and one in the mountains tour. The team's manager said the rider had repaid the squad's faith many times over. Roon credited his lead-out train and the medical staff who managed his recovery from the March crash. The winter programme begins at the training camp next month, and the squad's opening race of the new season is provisionally fixed for January.

[1] Title: Stadium Plans Unveiled for Regional Centre, Day: March 4, 2021 Content: The regional athletics federation has unveiled plans for a thirty-thousand-seat stadium, the drawings published on 3 March 2021 after two years of feasibility work. The stadium, planned for the old gasworks site, would host the region's championship ambitions and provide a permanent home for athletics. The federation's president said the centre would end the sport's dependence on borrowed football grounds. Funding would combine public money, naming rights, and the sale of the federation's current fields. Objections are expected from the residents of the neighbouring streets, whose light and noise concerns derailed an earlier scheme. The federation has opened a consultation running to the summer, and a planning application would follow if the response is favourable. The sport's national body has lent its support in writing. Local clubs welcomed the promise of a proper track, their athletes having trained on car parks through the winter lockdowns.
[2] Title: Ground Broken on Stadium Site, Day: June 8, 2022 Content: Ground was broken on the regional stadium yesterday, 7 June 2022, fifteen months after the plans were published, the delay spent in planning committee and a judicial review brought by the residents' association, which failed in the spring. The ceremony, performed by the federation's president with a mechanical spade, begins eighteen months of construction. The gasworks site's decontamination, finished in April, cleared the last obstacle. The stadium's steel is being rolled now, and the first columns rise in the autumn. The championship hosting, agreed with the national body last year, is confirmed for the summer of 2023, the dates to be fixed once the roof programme is certain. The contractor's compound has taken over the old fields, whose sale funded the decontamination. The residents' association has withdrawn its remaining objections on the strength of the noise bund now under construction.
[3] Title: Record Falls at New Stadium's Championships, Day: August 16, 2023 Content: The regional championships opened at the new stadium on 12 August 2023, the first meeting on the track that cost one hundred and ten million. Four days in, on 15 August 2023, the oldest record of the programme fell: the steeplechase mark, standing since the century's first years, lowered by four seconds by the home runner Nina Vasq. The championships' closing ceremony on 20 August 2023 closed six days of competition before crowds that filled the stadium to its rafters. The federation has declared the hosting a triumph and the track the fastest in the region. The stadium's public opening, a community meet, has been fixed for 4 May 2024, when the gates open to the clubs that trained through the winter on car parks. The president said the record proved the centre's worth already. Vasq's name goes on the stadium's wall of honour, the first name there.
