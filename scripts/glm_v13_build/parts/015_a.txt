The trust, split on the sale, voted its approval 214 to 96.

[1] Title: Region Awards the Summer Championships, Day: September 9, 2015 Content: The federation's council, meeting on 8 September 2015, awarded the 2017 summer athletics championships to the region's city, its bid having beaten two rivals on the second ballot. The organising committee formed within the month, chaired by the city's former mayor, and the federation's coordination commission paid its first visit 11-12 November 2015. The bid book promises a refurbished stadium, an athletes' village on the former rail goods yard, and a schools' programme reaching ninety thousand children before the opening ceremony. The costs, the bid book says, are four hundred and ten million, the village's later sale meant to repay a third. The goods yard, derelict since 1984, was transferred to the city in 2014 and sits behind hoardings already. The games are set for 4-13 August 2017. The losing cities' delegates praised the schools' programme, the element said to have swung the second ballot. The athletes' village site's archaeology — the yard sits on the medieval fairground — is to be sampled in the spring.

The games' dates, 4-13 August 2017, are not moved — the federation's commission, visiting 20-21 October 2016, was told the roof will be on by March and the village's towers topped out in January. The schools' programme, by contrast, is ahead: sixty thousand children reached by the autumn term of 2016, the ninety thousand promise likely to be passed by Easter. The ticket ballot, opened 1 November 2016, drew four hundred thousand applications in a week. The former mayor chairing the committee has staked his collection of rail memorabilia, he told the council, on the roof's March date.
[3] Title: Championships Close to Full Houses, Day: August 14, 2017 Content: The championships ended last night, 13 August 2017, the closing ceremony held before a stadium the refurbishment finished in May — six weeks late for comfort, the roof closed, the memories open. The home team's four medals, one of them gold to a graduate of the schools' programme's first cohort, exceeded the forecast of two. The former mayor's rail memorabilia, staked on the roof's March date and lost to April, was raffled for the athletes' benevolent fund. The coordination commission's final report, dated 10 November 2017, calls the games a credit delivered at a cost the bid book underplayed.

The announcement follows two years of quiet negotiation, the club's lease expiring in 2021 and the ground's owners, a pension fund, declining renewal. The new stadium's promise is ten thousand seats, artificial turf, and shared facilities with the county cricket board, the cricket club having signed a memorandum on 14 May 2018. The supporters' club, consulted at last on 20 May 2018 after the leak of the previous week, voted narrowly to engage rather than resist. The Old Field's terraces, the fans' century of concrete, will pass to the pension fund's developers, the club taking the memorial gates and the turf's topsoil, both to be relaid and rehung at the new place. The first season at the business park is priced at the same season tickets as the last at the Old Field, the chairman said, for one year only.
